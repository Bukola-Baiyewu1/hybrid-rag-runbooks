# On-call Escalation Policy

## Primary and secondary
The primary on-call engineer must acknowledge a page within 5 minutes. If the
page is not acknowledged within 15 minutes, it escalates automatically to the
secondary on-call engineer, and after 30 minutes to the engineering manager.

## Handover
On-call shifts change every Monday at 10:00 local time. The outgoing engineer
writes a handover note listing open incidents, silenced alerts, and risky
changes planned for the week.

## Escalating to other teams
Escalate database problems to the database owner, certificate and ingress
problems to the platform team, and suspected security incidents to the
security team through the security hotline, not through regular chat.
